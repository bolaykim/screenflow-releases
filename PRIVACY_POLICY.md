# Privacy Policy for Screenflow

**Effective Date:** November 20, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 20, 2026

## 1. Overview

Screenflow ("the App") is a local Windows desktop utility for managing multi-monitor layouts, display resolution, refresh rate, orientation, HDR settings, hardware DDC/CI brightness, monitor input sources, and display presets.

Your privacy is important to us. **Screenflow operates entirely offline on your device and does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.**

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect

Screenflow does **not**:
- Collect personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, IP address, or location).
- Require an account, sign-in, or registration.
- Transmit any telemetry, crash reports, usage analytics, or diagnostic data to any server.
- Connect to the internet or communicate with third-party services or advertising networks.
- Access your personal files, camera, microphone, contacts, or browsing activity.

## 3. Local Data Storage

To provide preset functionality, Screenflow stores your saved display configurations locally on your computer at:

`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Screenflow\config\presets.json`

This file contains only technical display configuration data (such as user-chosen preset names, monitor names, Windows display identifiers, resolutions, positions, refresh rates, orientation, HDR status, brightness levels, and monitor input selections). This data never leaves your device and can be deleted at any time by removing presets within the App or deleting the file directly.

## 4. System and Hardware Access

Screenflow uses standard Windows system interfaces (Windows Display APIs, WMI, and DDC/CI monitor communication) solely to detect your connected displays and apply the display settings you request. All hardware communication occurs strictly locally on your machine.

## 5. Third-Party Platforms (Microsoft Store)

If you download or install Screenflow through the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may collect certain diagnostic, installation, or usage data in accordance with the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement). We do not receive any personally identifiable information from Microsoft.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Because Screenflow does not collect any personal information from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of consent in your jurisdiction).

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if the App's functionality changes. Any updates will be reflected by the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
